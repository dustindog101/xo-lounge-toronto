[ 01 ] — Story

Two lounges in one: Habesha at heart, Toronto after dark.

Early evening it eats like family. Late night it moves like Queen Street. Same room, same warmth — the light just gets lower.

We cook the food we grew up sharing — teff injera, slow wots, tibs off a screaming-hot mitad — then we turn the music up and pour the second round of buna a little stronger.

XO started as a dare: what if a Habesha kitchen and a proper late-night lounge stopped pretending to be two different places? Weeknights smell like berbere and fresh roast. Weekends, DJ Abel hands off to live krar and keys, and the back room becomes somebody's birthday, engagement, or just-thursday.

No white-tablecloth stiffness. No velvet-rope coldness. Come hungry at 7, still here at 1 — that's the whole business plan.
